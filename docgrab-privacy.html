DocGrab privacy policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026. DocGrab is published by the developer named on its Chrome Web Store listing. Contact: apps@bluemind-software.ro.

Summary

DocGrab collects no data. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Nothing is sold.

What DocGrab stores

Nothing is sent to the developer. On your computer, Chrome keeps the extension's own settings and the latest scan of the page you chose (file names, link addresses, and which rows you ticked). That stays in the browser profile, with Chrome's extension storage. Downloaded files are written only to your Downloads folder.

What DocGrab sends over the network

  • Nothing to the developer, and nothing to an analytics or payment service.
  • When you download a file, Chrome requests that file from the site that hosts it, the same way a normal download does. DocGrab does not receive a copy.

Files

Downloads go to your Downloads folder, in a subfolder named after the site only if you leave that option on. DocGrab never uploads them.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell, share or transfer user data to anyone. There is no purchase and no account. The use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Remove the extension: Chrome then deletes DocGrab's local data from your profile. Files already saved in Downloads stay there until you delete them.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: apps@bluemind-software.ro.

Changes

Material changes will be announced in the extension's release notes and this page will be updated.
