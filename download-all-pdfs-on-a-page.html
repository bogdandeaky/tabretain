How to download all PDFs on a web page at once

Course pages, council minutes, product manuals and reports often link dozens of PDFs. Clicking each one is slow. Here are your options.

1. One by one (fine for a few files)

Right-click a link → Save link as…. Good for 3–5 files. Beyond that it gets tedious, and many files arrive with names like download(7).pdf.

2. The command line (for technical users)

With wget installed you can fetch every PDF linked from one page:

wget -r -l1 -nd -A pdf https://example.com/page

This does not work for pages that need you to be logged in, or that build their links with JavaScript, unless you add cookies and extra options.

3. A browser extension (easiest)

An extension works inside the page you already have open, so it sees the same links you do, including after you log in. Look for one that lets you choose which files to download, rather than grabbing everything.

Be fair to the site

Only download files you are allowed to use, and avoid hammering a site with hundreds of requests at once.
