How to restore your tabs after Chrome crashes

If Chrome crashed or reopened with an empty window, start with the recovery steps below.

1. Press Ctrl+Shift+T (Cmd+Shift+T on Mac)

This reopens the last closed window or tab. Press it several times to go further back. It works best right after the crash, before you open and close other windows.

2. Use History → Recently closed

Open the menu (⋮) → History. Under Recently closed Chrome often lists the whole window, for example "12 tabs". Click it to reopen all of them.

3. Search your full history

If the window is not listed, open chrome://history and search for words from the pages you had open. This finds pages one by one, so it is slow, but nothing is lost from history.

4. Next time: make Chrome reopen your tabs

Go to Settings → On start-up → Continue where you left off. This helps after a normal restart. It does not help when Chrome's own session files are damaged, or when the profile was reset.

When none of this works

Chrome keeps its session in a few files inside your profile. If those are overwritten (another crash, a second Chrome start, a profile repair), the list of open tabs is gone. The only reliable fix is a copy saved outside Chrome's session files, made before the crash. That is what a tab-saving extension does.
