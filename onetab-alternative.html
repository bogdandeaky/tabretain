OneTab alternative for automatic tab backups: TabRetain

If you want automatic backups of your open tabs without an account, TabRetain saves them locally every few minutes for restoration after a crash or restart.

ToolMain idea
OneTabYou click its button and your open tabs move into one list page, which frees memory. You reopen them from that list.
Session BuddyA session manager: save, browse and reopen sets of windows and tabs.
Tab Session ManagerOpen-source session manager that saves sessions, including automatic saves, for Chrome and Firefox.
TabRetainSaves every window and tab in the background, notices after Chrome restarts when tabs went missing, and reopens only the missing ones.

Features change. Check each tool's current store page before deciding.

When OneTab is the better fit

If your goal is to close lots of tabs to save memory and read them later, OneTab's one-click list is a simple, proven way to do that.

When you may want something else

If your goal is "never lose my open windows", you want saving that happens without clicking, plus a history of snapshots to go back to.

Moving your OneTab list

In OneTab choose Export URLs and copy the text. TabRetain can import that text, as well as Session Buddy and Tab Session Manager JSON exports.
