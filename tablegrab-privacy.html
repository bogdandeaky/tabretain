TableGrab privacy policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026. TableGrab is published by the developer named on its store listing. Contact: apps@bluemind-software.ro.

Summary

TableGrab collects no data. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Nothing is sold.

How it works

When you click the TableGrab button, it reads the tables on the page you are viewing, only on that tab and only at that moment. It turns the table you pick into a CSV, Excel or Markdown file, or copies it to your clipboard. All of this happens inside your browser.

What TableGrab stores

Nothing. It keeps no history and no settings between uses. Files you export are saved only to your Downloads folder.

What TableGrab sends over the network

Nothing. TableGrab makes no network requests. Table contents never leave your computer.

Permissions

  • activeTab and scripting: read the tables on the current tab, only after you click the button.
  • downloads: save the file you asked for to your Downloads folder.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell, share or transfer user data to anyone. The use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

There is no stored data to delete. Files you exported stay in your Downloads folder until you delete them.

Contact

Questions about privacy: apps@bluemind-software.ro.

Changes

Material changes will be announced in the extension's release notes and this page will be updated.
